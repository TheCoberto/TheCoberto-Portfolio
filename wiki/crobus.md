# Kingdom of Crobus Wiki

## Welcome to the Kingdom
Kingdom of Crobus is a community Minecraft survival server for Java and Bedrock. It plays like vanilla, with extras on top: a player-run economy, jobs, shops, land claims, factions and clans, optional PvP, and plenty of quality-of-life features. There's no single goal. Build, trade, fight or explore: play your way.

## Rules
1. Be respectful to other players.
2. No cheating or hacking: no unauthorized mods, exploits or unfair advantages.
3. No glitching or duping.
4. **Unclaimed land is fair game.** Griefing and stealing are allowed outside claims, so claim your base and be careful who you trust.
5. Don't be obnoxious, childish or rude. We want light competition, not toxicity.
6. **Go easy on lag.** Keep automatic farms, big redstone and heavy builds to a minimum, and give automatic farms an on/off switch.
7. **Stay active.** Inactive players may lose their roles, builds or claims.

Breaking the rules gets a temporary ban the first time and a permanent ban for repeat offenders. Cheating has immediate consequences.

## Joining
- **Java:** add `crobus.minecraft.best` under **Multiplayer → Add Server**.
- **Bedrock on phone, PC or tablet:** add the server under **Play → Servers** with the same address and port 19132.
- **Xbox, PlayStation or Switch:** sign into your Microsoft account in Minecraft, go to **Play → Friends → Add Friend** and add **KingdomCrobus**. The server is under **Joinable Friends**.

Java and Bedrock players share the same world, chat and economy. Bedrock players may notice small differences (combat feel, menus, the off-hand), which are crossplay limits rather than bugs.

## Getting Around
- `/warp <name>`: the warps above. The Warps NPC at The Oasis has them in a menu.
- `/spawn`: back to The Oasis.
- **Homes**: `/sethome <name>` saves a spot, `/home <name>` takes you back, `/homes` lists them and `/delhome <name>` deletes one. You get 5 homes (VIPs get 20).
- **Teleport to a friend**: `/tpa <player>` asks to teleport to them.
- **Random Teleport**: `/tpr` drops you somewhere random (and safe) inside the world border. Great for finding a fresh spot for a base.
- **World Border**: the Overworld and the Nether have a border, scaled 1:8 like Nether travel, so Nether highways still work but can't take you past the edge.
- **Area Titles**: a title pops up when you walk into a named area.
- **One Player Sleep**: only one player needs to sleep to skip the night and clear the weather.
- **Live Map**: the server has a live web map of the world, builds and online players. The link is in the Discord. Other players can see you on it too.

## Land Claims
Claims protect your builds: inside one, nobody else can break or place blocks, open your chests or use your doors and levers. **Anything outside a claim can be griefed or raided**, so claim your base early.
- **Make a claim**: hold a **golden shovel**, right-click one corner, then the opposite corner.
- `/trust <player>`: full access (build, break, chests, doors). Only for people you'd trust with your whole base.
- `/accesstrust <player>`: can use doors, buttons and levers, but not build or open chests.
- `/containertrust <player>`: can also open chests and furnaces, and ride and leash animals in your claim.
- Not sure? Start with the lower trust level. Giving `/trust` to the wrong player can cost you your base.
- Claims don't turn PvP off. That depends on whether you're Peaceful or a Fighter.

## Money
- `/bal` shows your balance. Ways to earn: **Jobs**, **Daily Rewards**, selling to the shop, trading with players, and winning parkour courses.
- **Server Shop** (`/shop`): buy and sell at server prices. Some items have limited stock that restocks over time. Prices move with supply and demand: selling lots of an item lowers its sell price by up to 20% (Ores hold their price longest; Farming, Mobs, iron and gold drop faster), and prices slowly drift back over a few hours.
- **Magic Shop**: a rotating shop with rare items that changes completely **every hour**. If you see something you want, grab it before it's gone.
- **Auction House** (`/ah`): sell to other players at your own price. Hold an item and type `/ah sell <price>`. Unsold items come back to you when the listing expires.
- **Player Shops** (ChestShop): put a sign on a chest to run your own shop:

  | Line | What to write |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Your name |
  | 2 | How many per trade, like `64` |
  | 3 | Prices: `B 10` (players buy for $10), `S 5` (players sell to you for $5), or `B 10 : S 5` for both |
  | 4 | The item, like `DIAMOND` |

  Keep your shop stocked and labeled clearly, and don't block public shop areas.
- **Jobs** pay you for normal play: mining, farming, chopping, hunting, fishing and more. `/jobs browse` shows them and `/jobs join <job>` joins one. The more you work, the higher your level and the better the pay. Tip: stick to one or two jobs to level faster, then sell what you gather.
- **Daily Rewards**: claim free money and items every day, week and month from the rewards menu. VIPs get bigger rewards. Make room in your inventory first.
- **Death Tax**: every death costs **1%** of your balance, however you die. It's gone for good, not given to anyone.
- **Mystical Slots** at The Oasis: the house keeps about 6.5% over time, so play for fun.

## PvP: Peaceful or Fighter
- You choose when you first join. Change it any time with `/pvpmenu` or the shield in the faction menu.
- **Peaceful**: nobody can hurt you, and you can't hurt anyone. Costs the **$100 Civilian tax** every hour. If you can't pay it, you're switched to Fighter.
- **Fighter**: you can fight other Fighters anywhere outside safe zones. No tax.
- 1-minute cooldown between switches, and you can't switch mid-fight.
- **Newbie Protection**: new players can't be hurt by other players for their first hour (a boss bar shows the time left). Picking Fighter ends it early, and `/newbie disable` does too. Once it's gone, it's gone.
- **Safe zones**: The Oasis (spawn), the Ice Bridge and Nether spawn. No PvP there.
- **Practice Area** (`/warp practice`): PvP with no item or XP loss.
- **The wilderness is dangerous**: travel with gear you can afford to lose, bring food, and go in groups.

## Factions and Clans
**Factions** are the three big powers of the kingdom, made and run by the server: 🔵 Azure Order, 🟢 Verdant Syndicate and 🔴 Crimson Dominion. You pick one in the faction menu, and each has its own traits (see **Faction Traits** below). Faction wars, tournaments and events are run by the server.

**Clans** are player-made groups for friends and allies (`/clan`). Make your own, invite members, pick a tag, and track kills, deaths and KDR. Clans can ally or become rivals, and clan battles are organized by players. The Kingdom Board on Discord shows every faction and clan.

You can be in a faction **and** a clan: the faction is the big picture, the clan is your crew.

## Out in the World
- **Loot Crates** are hidden around the map. Open one for random loot; finding an Elytra or Heavy Core is announced to everyone. **Holiday Crates** show up for the holidays, and **Spooky Crates** too.
- **Prank Buttons** are out in the wild. Push one at your own risk. They can't be broken or taken.
- **Skeleton Variants**: about 1 in 3 skeletons is special, with its own gear, attacks and loot. See **Skeleton Variants** below.
- **Whispers of the Void**: things that stalk, hide and watch you in the dark. See **Whispers of the Void** below.

## Mystical Slots
`/warp oasis`

Right-click a Slot Machine at The Oasis and pick a bet. Three reels each land on a number from 1 to 9, and **7 comes up three times as often as any other number**. The machine takes your bet first, then pays out:

| Roll | Example | Chance | Low Risk ($100) | High Roller ($10,000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jackpot**: 7 7 7 | 7 · 7 · 7 | 2.0% (1 in 49) | $1,777 | $177,777 |
| **Triple**: any other three of a kind | 4 · 4 · 4 | 0.6% (1 in 166) | $1,000 | $100,000 |
| **Pair**: two the same | 2 · 9 · 2 | 34.3% (about 1 in 3) | $150 | $15,000 |
| No match | 1 · 5 · 8 | 63.1% | nothing | nothing |

Yes, 7 7 7 really is more likely than any other triple: the 7s are loaded in its favor.

**How fair is it?** On average a spin pays back about **93.5%** of your bet, so the house keeps about **6.5%** over time. That's about the same as a real casino slot machine and a bit worse than roulette, while real-life blackjack played well keeps only about 0.5–2%. Spin for fun, not to get rich: over 100 Low Risk spins ($10,000) you'd expect to come out about $650 down, but one lucky jackpot can flip that.

- `/slotstop` shows the leaderboard: who's spent, won and hit the most.
- Jackpots are announced to everyone.
- You need the full bet in your balance to spin.

## Parkour
Courses near The Oasis pay money when you reach the finish. Step on the start to begin; checkpoints save your progress, so a fall sends you back to the last one instead of the start. There's no fall damage on the courses.

| Course | Made by | Checkpoints | Prize | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Puddle** | DrCrobus | 2 | $250 | Easy: a good first course |
| **Fred** | Ducky | 1 | $4,500 | Hard: big prize, tricky jumps |

- **Prize cooldown**: you can win each course's prize once every **12 hours**. You can still run it as often as you like.
- **Rules on a course**: no flying (Flight Tokens don't work, and starting a course turns token flight off), most commands are blocked, and mobs can't hurt you.
- `/pa leave` quits the course you're on.
- Finishing a course is announced to everyone, with your time.

## Whispers of the Void
Something lives in the dark. None of these creatures are announced, and most of them never attack. They're here to make you look over your shoulder.

| Creature | What you'll see | Dangerous? | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Shifter** | An ordinary mob (sheep, pig, cow, villager, zombie and more) that never attacks and **keeps looking at you** | **Yes.** 250 hearts, hits weaken you, and it blinds everyone nearby when it vanishes | If a mob won't stop staring, back off. ## Classes
Type `/class` (or compass menu → **Pick a Class**) to pick one. One class at a time; it stays through logging out and dying. After picking, you wait **5 minutes** to change again (your first pick is free). Powers marked "charged hit" only work on a fully charged, direct melee hit.

**What's a charged hit?** After each swing, your weapon takes a moment to recharge (the little sword icon under your crosshair on Java): about 0.6 seconds for a sword, about 1 second for an axe. Wait for it to fill and your hit is "charged": full damage, and class powers trigger. Spam-clicking or sweeping several mobs at once won't trigger them. Bedrock players don't see the icon, but the same timing applies: don't swing faster than about twice a second.

## Graves
When you die, your items go into a **grave** where you died instead of spilling on the ground, safe from lava, despawning and other players. Only you can open it. Graves last **3 hours**, then they're gone with everything inside, so head back quickly (and watch out for whatever got you).
