# Kingdom of Crobus Wiki

## Getting Started

### Welcome to the Kingdom
Kingdom of Crobus is a community Minecraft survival server for Java and Bedrock. It plays like vanilla, with extras on top: a player-run economy, jobs, shops, land claims, factions and clans, optional PvP, and plenty of quality-of-life features. There's no single goal. Build, trade, fight or explore: play your way.

### Joining
- **Java:** add `crobus.minecraft.best` under **Multiplayer → Add Server**.
- **Bedrock on phone, PC or tablet:** add the server under **Play → Servers** with the same address and port 19132.
- **Xbox, PlayStation or Switch:** sign into your Microsoft account in Minecraft, go to **Play → Friends → Add Friend** and add **KingdomCrobus**. The server is under **Joinable Friends**.

Java and Bedrock players share the same world, chat and economy. Bedrock players may notice small differences (combat feel, menus, the off-hand), which are crossplay limits rather than bugs.

### Starter Kit
The first time you join, you get a free **Starter Kit**:
- Stone Sword
- Stone Pickaxe
- Stone Axe
- Stone Shovel
- **Compass**: right-click it to open the server menu (Spawn, Home, Classes and more). See [Handy Commands](#handy-commands).

That's the whole kit. You start with **$0** and no armor or food, so earn your first money with [Jobs](#jobs) and claim your [Daily Rewards](#daily-rewards) for food and XP. You also start with an hour of [Newbie Protection](#newbie-protection) from other players.

### Your First Day
1. **Pick Peaceful or Fighter** when you first join. Not sure? Read [PvP: Peaceful or Fighter](#pvp-peaceful-or-fighter).
2. **Pick a class**: the class menu opens right after, or use `/class` later. See [Classes](#classes).
3. **Find a spot** with `/tpr`, then **claim it** with a golden shovel. See [Land Claims](#land-claims).
4. **Save it** with `/sethome base`. See [Homes](#homes).
5. **Join a job** with `/jobs browse` and start earning. See [Jobs](#jobs).
6. **Claim your [Daily Rewards](#daily-rewards)** every day.

Right-click a **compass** any time for a menu of the most-used commands, or see [Handy Commands](#handy-commands).

### Rules
1. Be respectful to other players.
2. No cheating or hacking: no unauthorized mods, exploits or unfair advantages.
3. No glitching or duping.
4. **Unclaimed land is fair game.** Griefing and stealing are allowed outside claims, so [claim your base](#land-claims) and be careful who you trust.
5. Don't be obnoxious, childish or rude. We want light competition, not toxicity.
6. **Go easy on lag.** Keep automatic farms, big redstone and heavy builds to a minimum, and give automatic farms an on/off switch.
7. **Stay active.** Inactive players may lose their roles, builds or claims.

Breaking the rules gets a temporary ban the first time and a permanent ban for repeat offenders. Cheating has immediate consequences.

### Getting Around

#### Warps and Spawn
- `/warp <name>`: the [Locations](#locations) above. The Warps NPC at The Oasis has them in a menu.
- `/spawn`: back to The Oasis.

#### Homes
`/sethome <name>` saves a spot, `/home <name>` takes you back, `/homes` lists them and `/delhome <name>` deletes one. The compass menu's **Home** button (or `/homemenu`) opens a list of your homes: click one to go there. You get 5 homes ([VIPs](#vip-perks) get 20).

#### Teleporting
- **To a friend**: `/tpa <player>` asks to teleport to them. They answer with `/tpaccept` or `/tpdeny`.
- **Random Teleport**: `/tpr` drops you somewhere random (and safe) inside the world border. Great for finding a fresh spot for a base.

#### The World
- **World Border**: the Overworld and the Nether have a border, scaled 1:8 like Nether travel, so Nether highways still work but can't take you past the edge.
- **Area Titles**: a title pops up when you walk into a named area.
- **One Player Sleep**: only one player needs to sleep to skip the night and clear the weather.
- **Live Map**: the server has a live web map of the world, builds and online players. The link is in the [Discord](#discord). Other players can see you on it too.

## Land and Protection

### Land Claims
Claims protect your builds: inside one, nobody else can break or place blocks, open your chests or use your doors and levers. **Anything outside a claim can be griefed or raided**, so claim your base early.

#### Making a Claim
Hold a **golden shovel**, right-click one corner, then the opposite corner. `/claimslist` shows your claims and how many claim blocks you have left, and `/abandonclaim` deletes the claim you're standing in.

#### Trusting Players
| Command | What they can do |
|---|---|
| `/accesstrust <player>` | Use doors, buttons and levers, but not build or open chests |
| `/containertrust <player>` | Also open chests and furnaces, and ride and leash animals in your claim |
| `/trust <player>` | Full access: build, break, chests, doors. Only for people you'd trust with your whole base |
| `/untrust <player>` | Take their access away |
| `/trustlist` | Who's trusted in the claim you're standing in |

Not sure? Start with the lower trust level. Giving `/trust` to the wrong player can cost you your base.

#### What Claims Protect Against
- Players you haven't trusted **can't drive boats or minecarts into your claim**, and can't scoop your animals or [pets](#pets) into one to carry them off.
- They **can't leash or unleash** mobs standing in your claim.
- **Books on lecterns** can be read by anyone but taken by nobody but you.
- **Every block is logged.** If something in your claim gets broken anyway, tell staff: they can see who did it and roll it back.

Claims don't turn PvP off. That depends on whether you're [Peaceful or a Fighter](#pvp-peaceful-or-fighter).

## Money and Items

### Earning Money
`/bal` shows your balance and `/baltop` the richest players. Ways to earn: [Jobs](#jobs), [Daily Rewards](#daily-rewards), selling to the [Server Shop](#server-shop), trading with players on the [Auction House](#auction-house) or in [Player Shops](#player-shops), and winning [Parkour](#parkour) courses. `/pay <player> <amount>` sends money to someone.

### Jobs
Jobs pay you for normal play: mining, farming, chopping, hunting, fishing and more. The more you work, the higher your level and the better the pay.
- `/jobs browse` shows them and `/jobs join <job>` joins one.
- `/jobs stats` shows your levels, and `/jobs leave <job>` quits one.
- Tip: stick to one or two jobs to level faster, then sell what you gather.
- Reach Miner level 25 to unlock [Vein Miner](#vein-miner).

### Shops and Trading

#### Server Shop
`/shop`: buy and sell at server prices. Some items have limited stock that restocks over time. Prices move with supply and demand: selling lots of an item lowers its sell price by up to 20% (Ores hold their price longest; Farming, Mobs, iron and gold drop faster), and prices slowly drift back over a few hours.

#### Magic Shop
A rotating shop with rare items that changes completely **every hour**. If you see something you want, grab it before it's gone.

#### Auction House
`/ah`: sell to other players at your own price. Hold an item and type `/ah sell <price>`. Unsold items come back to you when the listing expires.

#### Player Shops
Put a sign on a chest to run your own shop (ChestShop):

| Line | What to write |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your name |
| 2 | How many per trade, like `64` |
| 3 | Prices: `B 10` (players buy for $10), `S 5` (players sell to you for $5), or `B 10 : S 5` for both |
| 4 | The item, like `DIAMOND` |

Keep your shop stocked and labeled clearly, and don't block public shop areas.

### Daily Rewards
Claim free money and items every day, week and month from the rewards menu. [VIPs](#vip-perks) get bigger rewards. Make room in your inventory first.

### Death Tax
Every death costs **1%** of your balance, however you die. It's gone for good, not given to anyone.

### Vein Miner
Reach **Miner level 25** in [Jobs](#jobs) and you unlock vein mining: break one ore and the **whole vein** comes with it, up to 64 blocks. You'll get a message when it unlocks.
- `/veinminer` (`/vm`) turns it on or off. It's off until you turn it on.
- Works with an **iron, gold, diamond or netherite pickaxe**, on every ore: coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald, Nether quartz, Nether gold and **ancient debris**.
- **Sneak** while mining to break just one block.
- Every block still costs durability, so bring a pickaxe with Unbreaking or Mending. Fortune and Silk Touch apply to the whole vein.

### Special Items
| Item | Where to get it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Ancient Flight Token** | Server Shop, **$100,000**. Only 2 in stock for the whole server, restocking every 12 hours | Right-click for **15 minutes of flight**, anywhere. One at a time, and not on parkour courses. It keeps counting if you log out or change worlds |

**PvP tip:** a Flight Token is a great escape, but you can still be shot down. Landing in enemy territory with 10 seconds left is a classic way to lose your gear.

## Games and Fun

### Mystical Slots
`/warp oasis`

Right-click a Slot Machine at The Oasis and pick a bet. Three reels each land on a number from 1 to 9, and **7 comes up three times as often as any other number**. The machine takes your bet first, then pays out.

#### Payouts
| Roll | Example | Chance | Low Risk ($100) | High Roller ($10,000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jackpot**: 7 7 7 | 7 · 7 · 7 | 2.0% (1 in 49) | $1,777 | $177,777 |
| **Triple**: any other three of a kind | 4 · 4 · 4 | 0.6% (1 in 166) | $1,000 | $100,000 |
| **Pair**: two the same | 2 · 9 · 2 | 34.3% (about 1 in 3) | $150 | $15,000 |
| No match | 1 · 5 · 8 | 63.1% | nothing | nothing |

Yes, 7 7 7 really is more likely than any other triple: the 7s are loaded in its favor.

#### How Fair Is It?
On average a spin pays back about **93.5%** of your bet, so the house keeps about **6.5%** over time. That's about the same as a real casino slot machine and a bit worse than roulette, while real-life blackjack played well keeps only about 0.5–2%. Spin for fun, not to get rich: over 100 Low Risk spins ($10,000) you'd expect to come out about $650 down, but one lucky jackpot can flip that.

#### Slots Tips
- `/slotstop` shows the leaderboard: who's spent, won and hit the most.
- Jackpots are announced to everyone.
- You need the full bet in your balance to spin.

## Combat

### PvP: Peaceful or Fighter

#### Choosing a Side
- You choose when you first join. Change it any time with `/pvpmenu` or the shield in the faction menu.
- **Peaceful**: nobody can hurt you, and you can't hurt anyone. Costs the **$100 Civilian tax** every hour. If you can't pay it, you're switched to Fighter.
- **Fighter**: you can fight other Fighters anywhere outside safe zones. No tax.
- 1-minute cooldown between switches, and you can't switch mid-fight.

#### Newbie Protection
New players can't be hurt by other players for their first hour (a boss bar shows the time left). Picking Fighter ends it early, and `/newbie disable` does too. Once it's gone, it's gone.

#### Safe Zones
The Oasis (spawn), the Ice Bridge and Nether spawn. No PvP there. The [Practice Area](#practice-area) (`/warp practice`) has PvP with no item or XP loss.

#### Staying Alive
The wilderness is dangerous: travel with gear you can afford to lose, bring food, and go in groups. Every death costs the [Death Tax](#death-tax), and your items wait in a [grave](#graves).

Type `/class` (or compass menu → **Pick a Class**) to pick one. One class at a time; it stays through logging out and dying. After picking, you wait **5 minutes** to change again (your first pick is free). Powers marked "charged hit" only work on a [fully charged, direct melee hit](#whats-a-charged-hit).

#### What's a Charged Hit?
After each swing, your weapon takes a moment to recharge (the little sword icon under your crosshair on Java): about 0.6 seconds for a sword, about 1 second for an axe. Wait for it to fill and your hit is "charged": full damage, and class powers trigger. Spam-clicking or sweeping several mobs at once won't trigger them. Bedrock players don't see the icon, but the same timing applies: don't swing faster than about twice a second.

### Factions and Clans
You can be in a faction **and** a clan: the faction is the big picture, the clan is your crew. The Kingdom Board on [Discord](#discord) shows every faction and clan.

#### Factions
The three big powers of the kingdom, made and run by the server: 🔵 Azure Order, 🟢 Verdant Syndicate and 🔴 Crimson Dominion. Pick one in the faction menu (compass menu → **Factions**, or `/faction_select`) or by talking to a faction leader, and each has its own [traits](#faction-traits). Your first pick is free; after that, switching costs **$1000** and you wait **5 minutes** between changes. Faction changes may be locked during faction events. Faction wars, tournaments and events are run by the server.

#### Faction Traits
Each faction has small perks and one drawback. They follow your faction, so switching factions switches traits.

| Faction | Perks | Drawback |
|---|---|---|
| 🔵 Azure Order | Dolphin's Grace in water; 25% less fall damage | 10% more fire damage |
| 🟢 Verdant Syndicate | 20% chance bone meal isn't used up; crops near you sometimes grow faster; 15% chance of an extra harvest from fully grown crops; 3s Regeneration after eating (every 30s) | 5% less melee damage |
| 🔴 Crimson Dominion | Fire Resistance in the Nether; 3s Speed after a kill (every 10s) | 1 less hunger point from each meal |

#### Clans
Player-made groups for friends and allies (`/clan`). Make your own, invite members, pick a tag, and track kills, deaths and KDR. Clans can ally or become rivals, and clan battles are organized by players.

### Graves
When you die, your items go into a **grave** where you died instead of spilling on the ground, safe from lava, despawning and other players. Only you can open it. Graves last **3 hours** ([VIPs](#vip-perks) get 12), then they're gone with everything inside, so head back quickly (and watch out for whatever got you).

## Dangers of the Wild

### Out in the World
- **Loot Crates** are hidden around the map. Open one for random loot; finding an Elytra or Heavy Core is announced to everyone. **Holiday Crates** show up for the holidays, and **Spooky Crates** too.
- **Prank Buttons** are out in the wild. Push one at your own risk. They can't be broken or taken.
- **Skeletons** aren't always just skeletons. See [Skeleton Variants](#skeleton-variants).
- **Something watches you in the dark.** See [Whispers of the Void](#whispers-of-the-void).
- **Event Bosses**: the **Mutant Creeper** and the **Skeleton King** only show up at server events.

### Whispers of the Void
Something lives in the dark. None of these creatures are announced, and most of them never attack. They're here to make you look over your shoulder.

| Creature | Dangerous? |
|---|---|
| [Disguised Shifter](#disguised-shifter) | Not until it reveals itself |
| [The Shifter](#the-shifter) | **Yes** |
| [Shifted Players](#shifted-players) | **Yes**, it's the Shifter |
| [Shifter Presence](#shifter-presence) | Mostly scary |
| [Herobrine](#herobrine) | No |
| [Eyes in the Dark](#eyes-in-the-dark) | No |
| [Staring Animals](#staring-animals) | No |

#### Disguised Shifter
The Shifter's hiding form: it looks exactly like an ordinary mob.
- **What you'll see:** a sheep, pig, cow, chicken, wolf, villager, zombie, skeleton, spider or creeper that **keeps turning to look at you**. Even the hostile ones never attack.
- **Danger: not yet.** It can't hurt you while disguised, but it turns into [the Shifter](#the-shifter) if you stay within 3 blocks of it for about 2 seconds, or hit it.
- **What to do:** a mob that stares and never attacks is the giveaway. Back away and leave it alone: a disguise that nobody bothers disappears after about 3 minutes.

#### The Shifter
- **What you'll see:** a tall dark figure that appears where a [Disguised Shifter](#disguised-shifter) or [Shifted Player](#shifted-players) stood. It may stalk you from a distance, chase you down, or sniff you out.
- **Danger: yes.** 250 hearts, its hits weaken you, and it blinds everyone nearby when it vanishes.
- **What to do:** run, or fight it with a group and good gear. Sneaking can help.

#### Shifted Players
- **What you'll see:** a "player" in armor using a real player's name and skin (or one that's almost right), who walks up and stares.
- **Danger: yes.** It's [the Shifter](#the-shifter).
- **What to do:** check the name. Don't stand next to them.

#### Shifter Presence
- **What you'll see:** nothing, at first. Whistles, knocks, footsteps and torches going out, for 30-60 seconds in the dark.
- **Danger:** mostly scary. Maybe a jumpscare.
- **What to do:** find some light and keep moving.

#### Herobrine
- **What you'll see:** a figure 28-48 blocks away, watching. He moves when you look away, and freezes when you catch him. Torches near him go out.
- **Danger: no.** He never attacks.
- **What to do:** look back at him if you dare. Walk within 7 blocks or hit him and he's gone.

#### Eyes in the Dark
- **What you'll see:** pale, blinking eyes in dark places.
- **Danger: no.**
- **What to do:** walk within 12 blocks and they vanish.

#### Staring Animals
- **What you'll see:** a herd of farm animals freezes and stares at you in total silence.
- **Danger: no.**
- **What to do:** go to bed. It only happens if you haven't slept in over a day.

## Community and Extras

### Discord
In-game chat and the Discord chat channel are linked, so you can talk to players from your phone. Discord also has the Kingdom Board (factions and clans), the Market (shop news and trades), the Leaderboard, and the live map link. The same rules apply in both places.

### Voice Chat
The server supports **proximity voice chat**: you hear players near you, and they get quieter the farther away they are. It's optional.
- **Java only.** Install the **Simple Voice Chat** mod (Fabric, Forge, NeoForge or Quilt, from Modrinth or CurseForge) for your Minecraft version, then join as normal.
- Press **V** to open the voice chat menu: pick your microphone, set push-to-talk or voice activation, and mute players.
- You can make **groups** in the menu to talk to friends anywhere on the map.
- Bedrock players can't use it, so don't plan a raid where your Bedrock teammates can't hear you. Use [Discord](#discord) instead.

### Pets
Tamed wolves, cats, parrots and other pets get extra care here.
- **Sneak and double-click your pet** to open its **pet menu**.

- **Wolves stay out of creeper fights**: they won't attack creepers, so they won't get blown up next to you.
- Cats don't chase monsters, so they stay put in your base.
- Pets in your claim are protected: untrusted players can't leash them or ride off with them in a boat. Outside a claim, a Fighter can still hurt them, so keep your best dog at home before heading into PvP.

### Skins
Your skin carries over from your Minecraft account automatically. To wear a different one:
- `/skin <player name>`: copy any Minecraft player's skin, like `/skin Notch`.
- `/skin <url>`: use a skin image from the web.
- `/skin clear`: back to your own skin.
- `/skins`: a menu of skins to pick from.

Other players see the change straight away (you may need to rejoin to see it yourself). Pretending to be a staff member or another player to trick people breaks [rule 1](#rules).

### Stats
Look up stats for yourself, anyone else, or the whole server, from blocks mined to players killed. Results show in chat and can be shared with a click.
- `/stat` opens the stats help.
- `/stat <statistic> me`: your own, like `/stat player_kills me`.
- `/stat <statistic> player <name>`: someone else's.
- `/stat <statistic> top`: the server top 10, like `/stat deaths top` or `/stat mine_block diamond_ore top`.
- `/stat <statistic> server`: the server total.

Press **Tab** while typing to see every statistic. Good PvP ones: `player_kills`, `deaths`, `damage_dealt`, `damage_taken`. The **Leaderboard** channel on [Discord](#discord) shows the server's top players too.

## FAQ

### I died. Where are my items?
In your [grave](#graves), right where you died. Only you can open it, and it lasts 3 hours. Bring armor: whatever killed you may still be there. You also lost 1% of your balance to the [Death Tax](#death-tax).

### Someone griefed or robbed my base!
If it was **inside your claim**, tell staff: every block is logged and can be rolled back. If it was **outside a claim**, it's allowed ([rule 4](#rules)), and it can't be undone. [Claim](#land-claims) everything you care about with a golden shovel.

### Someone killed me, and I'm Peaceful!
Peaceful players can't be hurt by other players at all, so check `/pvpmenu`. If you couldn't pay the hourly $100 Civilian tax, you were switched to Fighter. Mobs, lava and fall damage still count.

### How do I make money fast?
Join one or two [jobs](#jobs) and stick with them. Mine with [Vein Miner](#vein-miner) once you hit Miner level 25, claim your [Daily Rewards](#daily-rewards), finish the [parkour courses](#parkour) (Fred pays $4,500), and sell to players on the [Auction House](#auction-house) for more than the shop pays.

### I'm on Bedrock and can't join.
Use the address with port **19132**. On Xbox, PlayStation or Switch, add **KingdomCrobus** as a friend and join from **Joinable Friends** (see [Joining](#joining)). Make sure your game is up to date.

### Why can't I hurt that player?
They're Peaceful, still under [Newbie Protection](#newbie-protection), or you're in a [safe zone](#safe-zones) (The Oasis, the Ice Bridge, Nether spawn).

### Do loot chests get emptied by whoever finds them first?
No. Every player gets **their own loot** from dungeon and structure chests, so you can still loot a structure someone else already raided.

### How do I report a cheater?
Tell staff on [Discord](#discord), with a screenshot or clip if you can. The server also runs anti-cheat, and **X-ray doesn't work here**: hidden ores show up as stone until you're right next to them.
