Apps

Apps you can use right here in your browser, with more on the way!

🎵 Andrew Jackson Bass Visualizer
Load a mix and a face, then watch it react to every bass drop.
 🎲 Dice Roller
Roll a six-sided die.
 🔢 Random Number Generator
Pick random whole numbers between a minimum and a maximum.
 ☕ Coffee Calculator
How much water to pour over your grounds for a weak, normal or strong brew.
 ☠️ Lethal
Pick any place on the map and see how dangerous it is: natural disasters, wildlife, crime, roads, pollution and health. Or browse rankings of the most dangerous places in the US.
 🧑‍🌾 Villagerify
Turn a photo of your face into a Minecraft villager. Download it as a villager texture, a player skin, a custom villager add-on or a 3D render.
